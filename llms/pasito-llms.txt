# Pasito

> Pasito is the AI-native workspace for employee benefits. The platform empowers benefits consultants, insurance carriers, and employers to simplify every part of the benefits journey — from proposal to enrollment to year-round employee engagement — using agentic AI workflows. Pasito supports over 6.6 million employees, manages $106B in premiums, and serves 3,600+ employers. Backed by Google, Y Combinator, and Core Innovation Capital.

Pasito's mission is to simplify the world of employee benefits so more families can live healthier lives and retire with dignity. The platform reduces manual back-office work, improves benefits utilization, and delivers personalized decision support to employees at scale. It integrates with 950+ TPA systems and 260+ HRIS platforms.

- [Platform Overview](https://pasito.ai/what-we-do): Full description of Pasito's agentic AI workspace, including the AI benefits assistant, benefits content management, proposal intake, analytics, and integrations.
